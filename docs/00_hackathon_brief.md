# Hackathon brief — OneAquaHealth IEEE Global Hackathon 2026 (summary in my own words)

## Logistics
- Platform: Devpost, online, public. Prize pool about $3,500 in cash.
- **Submission deadline: 1 Oct 2026, 9:30am IST (GMT+5:30).** Plan to submit the evening of 30 Sep.
- Eligibility shown on the page: students only, above legal age of majority, companies/professional organisations excluded, standard country exceptions.
- The page lists "Team required" but also says you can participate individually. **OPEN QUESTION: confirm in the full Rules.**
- Prizes: 1st $1,500, runner-up $1,000, second runner-up $500, two special mentions $250 each, IEEE certificates of merit (top 3) and participation certificates.
- Results and winning solutions are showcased on the OneAquaHealth Open Information Hub.

## Theme
Improve monitoring and sustainability of urban freshwater ecosystems using citizen science, AI, data platforms and digital health standards, following the One Health approach (ecosystem, animal and human health). Tagline: "From streams to systems: turning citizen science into actionable One Health intelligence."

## Tracks (a project must belong to one)
1. Citizen Science UX: guided workflows, simpler terms, better data accuracy, repeat engagement.
2. Data-to-Insight: dashboards, maps, trends, One Health insight summaries.
3. **AI-Supported Assessment (OUR PRIMARY TRACK):** use AI responsibly to support stream assessment without replacing human judgment. Build: AI prompts, validation checks, explainable AI, human-in-the-loop workflows.
4. Awareness and Storytelling.
5. Community and Gamification.
6. Resilience Informatics: early warning and predictive tools.
7. Digital Health Standards: FHIR models, AI agents, integration frameworks (our secondary track).

## What must be submitted
1. Track alignment: state the chosen track and how the project addresses the challenge.
2. Project description: problem, solution, target users, expected impact on ecosystem and human health.
3. Demo video, 3–5 minutes.
4. Public code repository (GitHub or equivalent) with source and documentation.
5. Working prototype, mockup or proof of concept.
6. Submit before the deadline.

## Judging criteria
- **Impact and alignment with the OneAquaHealth mission:** improves monitoring, protection, awareness or sustainability, and links ecosystem to human, animal and environmental health.
- **Innovation and creativity.**
- **Architecture:** quality of prototype, functionality, effective use of tools, APIs and data sources.
- **UX:** ease of use, clarity, accessibility.
- **Scale:** real-world implementation potential, scalability, integration with existing systems.

## Judges (mix of backgrounds)
Ecosystem scientist / OneAquaHealth programme coordinator, health-informatics and HL7/FHIR expert, IEEE members, EU project managers, product and programme managers. Expect them to value: real fidelity to the official app, standards (FHIR), responsible AI, and clear UX.

## Resources listed on the page
Slack, OneAquaHealth Community registration, the Citizen Science App, webinar sessions and mentorship (see Rules section).
